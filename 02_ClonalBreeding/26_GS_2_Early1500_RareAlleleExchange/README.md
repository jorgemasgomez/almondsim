GS-2 early1500: rare favourable allele exchange (experimental, revised)

32 parents; 20 compatible random crosses; 75 offspring/cross; genomic early selection retains 500 seedlings. Parent eligibility: HPT2, two phenotyping years. At most 8 original parents are replaced annually; new entrants cannot be removed in the same year. Up to 50 candidates are evaluated in descending replacement-index order.

Marker ranking: 50% historical importance + 50% current absolute estimated RRBLUP effect. Historical importance is the proportion of PREVIOUS experimental years in which the marker appeared among the 1,000 largest absolute nonzero effects. It is computed from raw effect rankings, not final persistence rankings, to avoid circular reinforcement. Current effect is abs(beta)/max(abs(beta)), so both components are 0 to 1. Select the 1,000 nonzero-effect markers with highest combined priority. In the first year, no history exists and ranking depends on current effect only. History resets for each replicate; no information from future years or other replicates is used.

Favourable allele orientation follows the current effect sign. Rarity weights are combined marker priority times (1 - starting favourable allele frequency in parents); weights and normalization stay fixed during annual exchanges. Parent index remains 50% standardized GEBV and 50% standardized weighted favourable allele copies. Accept only positive index changes that protect every last favourable allele copy among selected markers. No separate heterozygosity-loss threshold applies.

Annual trace files include all candidate-parent pairs: GEBVs, rarity scores, changes in each, total index change, lost last copies, eligibility, renewal limit and acceptance. Candidate traces record rejection reasons and parental mean GEBV before/after. Marker history, priority, effects and frequencies are saved too. This permits quantifying accepted GEBV losses and improving exchanges blocked by protection. Crossing and the other 25 schemes are unchanged.

00RUNME.R runs four sequential experimental replicates from saved burn-ins; outputs go outside Git. Preparation does not launch a run. The revised launcher fills missing columns before combining baseline and experimental results. Existing completed experiment files are not modified.
